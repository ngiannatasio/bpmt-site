DoorLoop vs TurboTenant (2026): Free Landlord App or Full Platform?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Nov 2026

TurboTenant is where many small landlords start, because its core features cost the landlord nothing. DoorLoop is where many end up once accounting, maintenance and owners start to pile up. The question is which side of that line you are on. Our author has used DoorLoop in his own operation; the TurboTenant side is based on TurboTenant's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, not hands-on use.

The short version

One to ten of your own units, mainly filling vacancies and collecting rent: TurboTenant, free or Pro. Managing for other owners, or you want accounting, maintenance and owner statements in one system: DoorLoop.

Where TurboTenant wins

Cost at small sizes. The free plan covers listings, applications, screening and online rent collection with no unit cap, and even Pro is only $199 a year for up to 10 units. Applicants normally pay for screening. Many tenants have already used TurboTenant, which helps adoption. Read the TurboTenant review →

Where DoorLoop wins

Depth. DoorLoop is a full property management platform: complete accounting, owner statements, maintenance workflows and free payments out to vendors and owners. TurboTenant covers the front half of being a landlord; DoorLoop covers the whole job, including properties you manage for others. Read the DoorLoop review →

The cost math

At 10 units, TurboTenant Pro is $199 a year; DoorLoop One starts at about $300 a year before its minimum spend. At 50 units, TurboTenant Pro is $699 a year against a DoorLoop starting price of about $1,500. TurboTenant is the cheaper tool at every size; DoorLoop costs more because it does more. DoorLoop now prices per unit, and every plan carries a minimum spend that DoorLoop does not publish, so get a quote for your unit count.

The verdict

If you self-manage a handful of units, start with TurboTenant and pay nothing until you need to. Once you are handling other owners' money, juggling maintenance across many units, or want real accounting in the same system, DoorLoop is the upgrade.

Frequently asked questions

Is TurboTenant really free compared with DoorLoop?

TurboTenant's core plan is free for landlords with no unit cap; applicants usually pay for screening and tenants pay 3.49% for card payments. DoorLoop has no free plan and starts at $2.50 per unit per month plus a minimum spend.

When should I switch from TurboTenant to DoorLoop?

When you start managing for other owners, need owner statements, or want maintenance and full accounting in one place rather than across several tools.

Does TurboTenant have accounting?

Yes, on its Pro plan, aimed at your own taxes (Schedule E). It is not built for owner statements or trust accounting.

Outgrown landlord apps?

DoorLoop adds full accounting and owner statements, and most landlords are running within a day.
