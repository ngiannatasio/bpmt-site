DoorLoop Pricing (2026): New Per-Unit Plans, Fees and What You Pay

Research-based guide by an active real estate operator · Last updated Nov 2026

DoorLoop has changed how it charges. New customers no longer see the Starter, Pro and Premium plans; instead there are three plans priced per unit, per month. Here is what the new pricing says, what it leaves out, and how to work out your own number. Our author has used DoorLoop in his own operation.

The short version

Most landlords and small managers: DoorLoop One, from $2.50 per unit per month, dropping to $1.50 per unit above 100 units. Want help handling tenant calls, texts and questions: DoorLoop Connect, from $4.00 per unit per month, with free incoming ACH. DoorLoop Multiply, from $5.00 per unit, is invite-only. Every plan carries a minimum spend and unit count that DoorLoop does not publish, and paying yearly saves up to 25%.

What the new pricing leaves out

Two things you need for a real number are not on the page. First, every plan says a minimum spend and minimum units apply, with no figures; for a small portfolio that minimum may well set your price rather than the per-unit rate. Second, there are no separate month-to-month and yearly per-unit prices. Ask for a quote with your exact unit count and billing term. If you are already a DoorLoop customer, DoorLoop says you can keep your current plan and pricing.

What a year might cost

A 6-unit landlord on DoorLoop One: about $15 a month, or $180 a year, at the starting price. At this size the unpublished minimum spend is likely to decide what you actually pay.

A 25-unit operator on DoorLoop One: about $62.50 a month, or $750 a year, before any minimum. On Connect the same portfolio starts at about $100 a month.

A 150-unit company on DoorLoop One: if the $1.50 rate applies to units above 100, about $325 a month, or $3,900 a year, before any minimum.

Is DoorLoop worth the price?

If all you need is to collect rent from a few units, free tools like TurboTenant or Avail cost less. DoorLoop earns its price when you want full accounting, owner statements and maintenance in one place, with onboarding that does not take weeks. If you are deciding between DoorLoop and the bigger names, see DoorLoop vs Buildium and DoorLoop vs AppFolio; if you are watching costs, see DoorLoop vs TenantCloud or DoorLoop vs RentRedi.

How to pay less

  • Pay yearly: DoorLoop says that saves up to 25%.
  • Start on DoorLoop One unless you specifically want help with tenant calls and texts; it already includes the old Premium features.
  • If you are on an older Starter, Pro or Premium plan, compare a quote before switching, since you can keep your current pricing.
  • Get the minimum spend in writing for your unit count before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does DoorLoop cost per month?

DoorLoop One starts at $2.50 per unit per month ($1.50 per unit above 100), DoorLoop Connect at $4.00 and invite-only DoorLoop Multiply at $5.00, plus a minimum spend and unit count that DoorLoop does not publish. Prices as of November 2026.

What happened to DoorLoop Starter, Pro and Premium?

DoorLoop replaced them with per-unit plans. It says everything that used to sit behind Premium, including the accounting depth, the API, unlimited users and unlimited storage, is now in DoorLoop One, and that existing customers can keep their current plans and pricing.

Does DoorLoop have a free plan or free trial?

DoorLoop does not list a free plan or a self-serve free trial. Its pricing page offers a free demo.

Who pays DoorLoop’s rent payment fees?

Going by DoorLoop’s plan comparison table, incoming ACH payments cost $1.50 on DoorLoop One and are free on Connect and Multiply. Applicant screening is $35 on every plan.

Is DoorLoop cheaper than Buildium?

It depends on your size. Buildium Essential starts at a flat $65 a month and Growth at $202. DoorLoop One starts at $2.50 per unit per month plus an unpublished minimum spend, so at 20 units its starting price is about $50 a month before that minimum.
