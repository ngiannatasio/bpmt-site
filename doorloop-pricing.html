DoorLoop Pricing (2026): Plans, Fees and What You Actually Pay

Research-based guide by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

DoorLoop's headline price is easy to find. What it costs you in practice depends on three things: how many units you have, whether you pay monthly or yearly, and which transaction fees you pass to tenants. Here is the full picture, with the fees most pricing pages leave out. Our author has used DoorLoop in his own operation.

The short version

Up to 10 units: Starter, $69 a month billed yearly ($99 month to month). More than 10 units: Pro, $149 a month billed yearly ($189 month to month). Want transaction fees waived and API access: Premium, $209 a month billed yearly. Paying yearly saves about 12–30%, depending on the plan.

What a year really costs

A 6-unit landlord on Starter, billed yearly: $828 a year in subscription. If tenants pay rent by bank transfer and you pass the $2.49 fee to them, your cost stays at $828 plus the one-time $98 in setup fees.

A 25-unit operator on Pro, billed yearly: $1,788 a year, or about $6 per unit per month. At this size the multi-entity accounting and QuickBooks sync are usually what you are paying for.

Is DoorLoop worth the price?

If all you need is to collect rent from a few units, free tools like TurboTenant or Avail cost less. DoorLoop earns its price when you want full accounting, owner statements and maintenance in one place, with onboarding that does not take weeks. If you are deciding between DoorLoop and the bigger names, see DoorLoop vs Buildium vs AppFolio; if you are watching costs, see DoorLoop vs TenantCloud.

How to pay less

  • Pay yearly instead of monthly: about $360 a year saved on Starter, $480 on Pro.
  • Pass ACH fees to tenants, or move to Premium if you would otherwise absorb them on many units.
  • Watch for DoorLoop's periodic welcome offers on the first months and onboarding fees.
  • Stay on Starter until you genuinely pass 10 units.

Frequently asked questions

How much does DoorLoop cost per month?

Starter is $69 a month billed yearly or $99 month to month and covers up to 10 units. Pro is $149 billed yearly or $189 monthly. Premium is $209 billed yearly or $239 monthly. Prices as of October 2026.

Does DoorLoop have a free plan or free trial?

DoorLoop does not list a free plan. Its pricing page offers a free demo rather than a self-serve free trial.

Who pays DoorLoop’s rent payment fees?

By default tenants pay the ACH fee ($2.49 on Starter, about $1 on Pro, free on Premium) and the 3.25% card fee. Landlords can choose to absorb them.

Is DoorLoop cheaper than Buildium?

At entry level they are close: Buildium Essential starts at $62 a month and DoorLoop Starter at $69 billed yearly. Both add payment and setup fees, so compare the total for your unit count. Buildium’s Growth plan starts at $192 a month versus DoorLoop Pro at $149 billed yearly.
