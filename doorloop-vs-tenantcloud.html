DoorLoop vs TenantCloud: Which All-in-One Platform Fits?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

DoorLoop and TenantCloud both promise everything in one place: rent collection, maintenance, screening and real accounting. TenantCloud is cheaper on paper; DoorLoop is easier to get running. Which matters more depends on your time and your portfolio.

The short version

Tight budget, comfortable with some setup: TenantCloud. You want to be up and running fast with fewer surprises: DoorLoop. Over roughly 60 leases or managing several entities: DoorLoop Pro.

Where TenantCloud wins

Price. At 10 to 60 leases, TenantCloud's annual plans cost a fraction of DoorLoop's, and you still get accounting, listings with syndication and tenant-paid screening. A free trial lets you test it properly. Read the TenantCloud review →

Where DoorLoop wins

Onboarding and day-to-day polish. DoorLoop's guided setup is the easiest we have seen, and its Pro plan adds multi-entity accounting, bank sync, QuickBooks sync, vendor payments and 1099s, which matter as you professionalize. Support options are broader too. Read the DoorLoop review →

The real trade-off

TenantCloud saves money but asks more of you: tier limits, lease caps and per-payment fees take working out. DoorLoop costs more but asks less. If your time is worth more than the price difference, DoorLoop usually wins.

The verdict

Budget-first landlords should trial TenantCloud. If you have tried cheaper tools before and abandoned the setup halfway, DoorLoop is the one most likely to stick.
