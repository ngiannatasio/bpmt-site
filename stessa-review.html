Stessa Review: Free Rental Accounting With Banking Built In

Research-based review by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

Stessa started as free rental-property accounting and has grown into a landlord platform with banking, rent collection and screening, while keeping its bookkeeping roots. It is associated with Roofstock, whose branding appears on its site. This review is built from Stessa's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, compared against the platforms we cover on this site. We have not run our own portfolio on it.

4.0 /5

The short version

The best free option if your main headache is bookkeeping. Unlimited properties, automatic bank feeds and investor-style reports on the free plan. Management features are lighter than a full property management platform.

What Stessa does

Stessa connects to your bank accounts and categorizes rental income and expenses per property, producing reports such as income statements and, on paid plans, a Schedule E report. It also offers online rent collection, tenant screening, maintenance tracking, vacancy marketing and its own cash-management accounts. A separate full-service management option is offered through a partner.

Strengths and weaknesses

What works

  • Strong free plan with unlimited properties
  • Accounting-first: bank feeds and investor-grade reports
  • Paid plans are inexpensive
  • Banking, rent collection and screening in the same place

Where it falls short

  • Not a full property management system for managing other owners
  • Schedule E report and legal forms require a paid plan
  • Lease templates cost extra on the free plan
  • Banking is through a partner bank, not Stessa itself

How it compares

Against Baselane: both pair banking with bookkeeping. Stessa leans toward investor reporting and analysis; Baselane toward banking per property. See our Baselane review.

Who it is for

Self-managing investors with a handful to a few dozen units whose biggest pain is keeping clean books for taxes and lenders.

The verdict

Stessa is the easiest recommendation for free rental accounting. Pair it with a rent-collection tool, or move to a full platform once operations, not bookkeeping, are the bottleneck.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stessa really free?

Yes. The Essentials plan is free with unlimited properties, bank feeds, basic reports, screening, maintenance tracking and online rent collection. Lease templates cost $15 each on the free plan.

Is Stessa a bank?

No. Stessa says it is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank; its banking is provided by Thread Bank.

Stessa or DoorLoop?
