Rentec Direct vs Buildium: Trust Accounting for Small Property Managers

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

If you manage properties for other owners, you need trust accounting and owner statements, and that narrows the field quickly. Buildium is the established name; Rentec Direct is the budget-friendly alternative that a lot of small managers overlook.

The short version

Small PM company watching costs: Rentec Direct's PM edition. Growing PM company that wants a larger ecosystem, more polish and phone support: Buildium Growth. Self-managing landlord with no outside owners: you probably need neither; see our under-10-units guide.

Where Rentec Direct wins

Cost. For a small management company, Rentec's PM edition delivers trust accounting and an owner portal at a lower monthly price, with cheaper screening and very low incoming ACH costs. No setup fees or long-term contracts. Read the Rentec Direct review →

Where Buildium wins

Polish and breadth. Buildium has a more modern interface, a larger integration ecosystem, live phone support from the Growth plan up, and more advanced reporting and automation at higher tiers. It is also the more common choice, which helps when hiring staff who already know the software.

Watch the fees on both

Headline prices are only part of the cost. On Buildium, per-payment EFT fees, extra bank accounts and e-signature charges add up on the Essential plan. On Rentec, the price rises with unit count. Price out your actual portfolio on both before committing.

The verdict

For a small property manager who values cost over polish, Rentec Direct is the smarter buy. If you are growing quickly, want stronger support and integrations, and can absorb Buildium Growth's price, Buildium is the safer long-term platform.
