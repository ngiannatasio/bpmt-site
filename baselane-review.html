Baselane Review: Banking and Bookkeeping Built for Landlords

Research-based review by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

Baselane approaches landlord software from the banking side: separate accounts for each property, automatic bookkeeping on top, and rent collection that lands money straight in those accounts. This review is built from Baselane's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, compared against the platforms we cover on this site. We have not run our own portfolio on it.

Baselane is a financial technology company and is not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided by Thread Bank, Member FDIC.

4.0 /5

The short version

A smart, free way to separate every property’s money and get bookkeeping almost automatically. Light on day-to-day management features such as maintenance workflows.

What Baselane does

Baselane gives you as many checking and savings accounts as you want, one per property if you like, and categorizes transactions into property-level bookkeeping with reports such as cash flow, NOI, rent roll and a Schedule E tax package. It collects rent online, with reminders, late fees and autopay, and syncs with QuickBooks and Xero.

Strengths and weaknesses

What works

  • Free unlimited bank accounts, one per property
  • Bookkeeping that largely runs itself
  • Schedule E tax package and lender-friendly reports
  • QuickBooks and Xero sync

Where it falls short

  • Not a full property management platform
  • Tenants pay card fees; ACH fee unless you absorb it
  • Automation features need the paid Smart plan
  • Banking is through a partner bank

The verdict

If commingled money and messy books are your problem, Baselane’s free plan is one of the cleanest fixes available. For full management, add or move to a platform built for operations.

Frequently asked questions

Is Baselane free?

The Core plan is $0 and includes unlimited checking and savings accounts, bookkeeping and rent collection. Smart costs $20 a month on an annual plan and adds automation such as auto-tagging and receipt matching.

Is Baselane a bank?

No. Baselane is a financial technology company; its banking services are provided by Thread Bank, Member FDIC.

What does Baselane charge for rent payments?

ACH rent is about $2 per payment, which you can absorb or pass to tenants. Card payments carry a 3.49% fee paid by tenants.
