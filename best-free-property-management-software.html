Best Free Property Management Software for Landlords (2026)

Research-based guide by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

"Free" property management software is real, but it is never free for everyone. Somebody pays: usually your applicants through screening fees, your tenants through payment fees, or you once you need features behind the paywall. Here is what each option really costs and when free stops being the right answer.

The short version

Best free plan overall: TurboTenant. Best free plan for leases in strict states: Avail. Best low-cost paid plan: RentRedi. When you need real accounting: DoorLoop.

Truly free: TurboTenant and Avail

Both offer free plans with no unit cap that cover listings, applications, screening and online rent collection. TurboTenant's paid tiers are cheap flat annual prices; Avail's free plan includes state-specific leases but tenants pay $2.50 per bank-transfer rent payment, and its upgrade is charged per unit. Our TurboTenant vs Avail comparison breaks down which to pick.

Who actually pays on a "free" plan

  • Applicants usually pay for screening, typically $35 to $55 per report.
  • Tenants usually pay card fees of about 3.5%, and sometimes a small ACH fee.
  • You pay once you need leases, e-signatures, accounting or faster payouts.

Free accounting-first options: Stessa and Baselane

If bookkeeping is your main need, Stessa and Baselane both have free plans built around clean per-property books and banking. Innago is another free management option with no paid tiers.

Cheap, not free: RentRedi and TenantCloud

If you know you will want the full feature set, a low-cost paid plan can beat a free one. RentRedi includes everything for about $12 a month on its annual plan, with unlimited units. TenantCloud starts at about $15 a month annually and includes real accounting. See RentRedi vs TurboTenant for the free-versus-flat decision.

When free stops making sense

Free tools are built around filling vacancies and collecting rent. Once bookkeeping across several units becomes a monthly chore, or you start managing for family or partners, you need proper accounting and owner statements. That is the point to move to a full platform such as DoorLoop, or Rentec Direct if you manage other owners' money on a budget.

The verdict

Start free with TurboTenant or Avail if you own a few units. Move to RentRedi when you want everything in one cheap plan, and to DoorLoop when accounting becomes real work. Choose for the portfolio you expect to own a year from now.
