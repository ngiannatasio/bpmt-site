RentRedi vs TurboTenant: Flat Price or Free Plan?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

RentRedi and TurboTenant are aimed at the same person: a landlord who owns their rentals and manages them personally. The difference is philosophy. TurboTenant starts free and sells upgrades; RentRedi charges one flat price and includes everything.

The short version

One to three units and you want to pay nothing: TurboTenant's free plan. Five or more units, or you want every feature without comparing tiers: RentRedi on the annual plan. Managing for other owners: neither; see DoorLoop or Rentec Direct.

Where RentRedi wins

Simplicity and value as you grow. Every feature is in one plan, there are no per-unit fees, and the annual price ($144 a year) undercuts TurboTenant's Pro plan. Its mobile apps are a genuine focus for landlords who manage from their phone. Read the RentRedi review →

Where TurboTenant wins

Getting started for nothing. The free plan covers listings, screening and rent collection with no unit cap, so a landlord with one or two units may never need to pay. TurboTenant also has a very large user base, so many tenants already know it. Read the TurboTenant review →

The cost math

At one to ten units, RentRedi annual costs $144 a year and TurboTenant Pro $199 a year, with TurboTenant free if you can live without paid features. Above ten units TurboTenant's paid tiers step up while RentRedi stays flat, which widens RentRedi's advantage. If you would pay RentRedi month to month ($29.95), TurboTenant is cheaper.

The verdict

If you are happy on a free plan, start with TurboTenant. If you know you want accounting and the full feature set, and you will commit to a year, RentRedi is the better value, and it gets better the more units you own.
