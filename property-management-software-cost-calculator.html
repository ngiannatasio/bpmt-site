Frequently asked questions

How much does property management software cost per unit?

It ranges from free (TurboTenant, Avail, Innago, Stessa and Baselane core plans) to a few dollars per unit per month for full platforms. Flat-priced tools like RentRedi get cheaper per unit as you grow; per-unit tools like Avail Unlimited Plus get more expensive.

What costs does the calculator leave out?

Payment processing, screening, e-signature and setup fees. Tenants and applicants pay many of these by default, but some fall on the landlord.
