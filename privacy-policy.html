Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Best Property Management Tools ("we," "us," or "this site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit bestpropertymanagementtools.com.

Information We Collect

We collect limited information through standard web analytics tools, which may include your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and general location (city/region level, not precise location). This is used to understand how visitors use the site and to improve content.

If you subscribe to any email list or contact us directly, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name and email address.

Cookies

This site may use cookies from analytics providers (such as Google Analytics) and from affiliate tracking partners (such as Impact and CJ Affiliate) to measure traffic and track referrals when you click an affiliate link. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended if you do.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you click through and make a purchase or sign up for a service, at no additional cost to you. Affiliate partners may set their own cookies to track referrals; their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for hosting (Vercel), analytics (Google Analytics), and affiliate tracking (Impact, CJ Affiliate). These providers may collect data independently in accordance with their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely to operate, maintain, and improve this site — including understanding which content is useful to visitors and measuring the performance of affiliate partnerships. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

Analytics data is generally retained according to the default retention settings of the tools we use (e.g., Google Analytics). Any information you provide directly to us (such as through a contact form) is retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry.

Your Choices

You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics tracking using tools such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You may also contact us directly to ask what information, if any, we hold about you.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the contact information listed on this site.
