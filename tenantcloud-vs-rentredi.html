TenantCloud vs RentRedi (2026): Lease-Based Tiers or One Flat Price?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Nov 2026

TenantCloud and RentRedi both sell paid, all-in-one landlord software with accounting included, and neither has a free plan. TenantCloud prices by how many active leases you have; RentRedi charges one flat price for unlimited units. This comparison is research-based, built from each company's published pricing and features plus independent reviews; we have not run our own portfolio on either.

The short version

Owner-landlord who wants the lowest price and everything included: RentRedi on the annual plan. You want bank reconciliation and tax reports, or an owner portal later: TenantCloud Pro or Business. Managing for other owners at any real scale: neither; see DoorLoop or Rentec Direct.

Where RentRedi wins

Price and simplicity. $144 a year on the annual plan covers unlimited properties, tenants and teammates with every feature included, so there are no tiers to outgrow. Its mobile apps are a core strength. Read the RentRedi review →

Where TenantCloud wins

Accounting depth and a real trial. TenantCloud's Pro tier adds bank reconciliation and tax reports, its Business plan adds owner management, and it lists properties with syndication. You can test it free for 14 days before paying. Read the TenantCloud review →

The cost math

At 10 leases, TenantCloud Starter is $180 a year against RentRedi's $144. At 30 leases it is $350, and at 60 leases $600, while RentRedi stays at $144. RentRedi is cheaper at every size on annual billing; TenantCloud's extra cost buys deeper accounting on Pro. TenantCloud's own pricing page shows two different monthly prices for Starter, so confirm before you buy.

The verdict

For most owner-landlords, RentRedi is the better value, and it gets better the more units you add. Choose TenantCloud if reconciliation and tax reports in the same tool matter to you, or if you expect to need its owner portal.

Frequently asked questions

Is RentRedi cheaper than TenantCloud?

Yes on annual billing: RentRedi is $144 a year with unlimited units, while TenantCloud runs $180 a year for 10 leases, $350 for 30 and $600 for 60.

Does TenantCloud or RentRedi have a free plan?

Neither. TenantCloud offers a 14-day free trial; RentRedi charges at signup with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Which has better accounting, TenantCloud or RentRedi?

TenantCloud, on its Pro tier, which adds bank reconciliation and tax reports. RentRedi includes accounting and reporting in its single plan.

Managing for other owners?
