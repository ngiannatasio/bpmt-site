DoorLoop vs Buildium (2026): Per-Unit Pricing or Flat Plans?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Nov 2026

DoorLoop and Buildium are the two platforms small and mid-size managers compare most. Both now cover rent collection, maintenance, screening and real accounting, but they price differently: DoorLoop per unit, Buildium in flat starting tiers. Our author has used DoorLoop in his own operation; the Buildium side is based on Buildium's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, not hands-on use.

The short version

Up to roughly 20–30 units and you want the easiest setup: DoorLoop. Growing management company that wants mature trust accounting and a free trial first: Buildium, usually on Growth. Either way, price both for your exact unit count.

Where Buildium wins

Accounting depth and a proper trial. Buildium has long been the pick for portfolios where trust accounting and owner reporting are central, and you can test any plan free for 14 days without a card. Its flat starting price can also work out cheaper for larger portfolios than paying per unit. The catch is fees on Essential and a big jump to Growth at $202 a month. See Buildium alternatives.

Where DoorLoop wins

Onboarding and what is included. DoorLoop's guided setup is the easiest we have seen, and its entry plan now includes the accounting depth, API and unlimited users that used to cost extra. On Connect, incoming ACH is free, and payments out to vendors and owners are free on every plan. Read the DoorLoop review →

The cost math

At 20 units, DoorLoop One starts at about $50 a month against Buildium Essential at $65. At 80 units DoorLoop One starts at about $200 a month, while Buildium Essential still starts at $65, though most portfolios that size need Growth at $202. Add Buildium's per-payment and bank account fees on Essential, and DoorLoop's unpublished minimum spend, before you decide; ask DoorLoop for a quote at your unit count.

The verdict

For a landlord or small manager who wants to be collecting rent in a day or two, DoorLoop is the easier choice. For an established management company that leans on trust accounting, Buildium Growth is the steadier pick. If you are in between, take Buildium's free trial and DoorLoop's demo in the same week and compare real quotes.

Frequently asked questions

Is DoorLoop cheaper than Buildium?

It depends on size. DoorLoop One starts at $2.50 per unit per month plus a minimum spend; Buildium Essential starts at $65 a month and Growth at $202. Small portfolios often start cheaper on DoorLoop, larger ones on Buildium, so compare quotes.

Does Buildium have a free trial?

Yes. Buildium offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card, and you can convert to Essential, Growth or Premium. DoorLoop offers a free demo instead.

Which is easier to set up, DoorLoop or Buildium?

DoorLoop. Its guided onboarding is the easiest we have seen; Buildium rewards time invested and requires onboarding for new Growth and Premium customers.
