TurboTenant Alternatives (2026): Free and Paid Options Compared

Research-based guide by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

TurboTenant is a strong free tool, but landlords outgrow it or bump into its limits: accounting only on the Pro plan, card fees that land on tenants, and paid tiers that step up with unit count. Here is where to go depending on what you need next.

The short version

Want another free option: Avail or Innago. Want everything for one low price: RentRedi. Need real accounting and owner statements: DoorLoop. Mostly need bookkeeping: Stessa or Baselane.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative to TurboTenant?

Avail is the closest swap, with state-specific lease templates on its free plan. Innago is also free with no paid tiers.

Is RentRedi cheaper than TurboTenant?

On an annual plan RentRedi costs about $144 a year with unlimited units. TurboTenant Pro is $199 a year for up to 10 units and rises with unit count, though TurboTenant’s core plan is free.

When should I move from TurboTenant to DoorLoop?

When you need full accounting, owner statements or maintenance workflows in one place, typically once bookkeeping across several units becomes a monthly chore.
