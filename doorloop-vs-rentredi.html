DoorLoop vs RentRedi (2026): Full Accounting or One Flat Price?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Nov 2026

RentRedi and DoorLoop both collect rent, take maintenance requests and keep the books, but they are priced for different jobs. RentRedi charges one flat price for unlimited units and is aimed at owner-landlords. DoorLoop prices per unit and is built to run a management operation, including properties you manage for other owners. Our author has used DoorLoop in his own operation; the RentRedi side is based on RentRedi's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, not hands-on use.

The short version

You own your rentals and want the lowest price: RentRedi on the annual plan. You manage for other owners, or need owner statements and deeper accounting: DoorLoop. Not sure yet: RentRedi's 30-day money-back guarantee and DoorLoop's free demo both let you look before you commit.

Where RentRedi wins

Price and simplicity. One plan includes every feature, with unlimited properties, tenants and teammates and no per-unit fees. On the annual plan that is $144 a year whether you own 3 units or 30. Its landlord and tenant mobile apps are a real focus, which suits landlords who manage from their phone. Read the RentRedi review →

Where DoorLoop wins

Running a business, not just a few rentals. DoorLoop handles owner accounting and the full management workflow, its entry plan now includes the accounting depth, API and unlimited users that used to cost extra, and payments out to vendors and owners are free. Its guided onboarding is the easiest we have seen. Read the DoorLoop review →

The cost math

RentRedi's annual plan costs $144 a year at any size. DoorLoop One starts at about $300 a year for 10 units and $1,200 a year for 40 units, before its minimum spend. RentRedi is cheaper at almost every size; what DoorLoop's price buys is owner accounting and a platform built for managing other people's properties. See DoorLoop pricing for the full breakdown.

The verdict

If you own your rentals and want everything for one low price, RentRedi is hard to beat. If you manage for other owners, need owner statements, or are building a management company, DoorLoop is the better fit, and the price difference is the cost of that capability.

Frequently asked questions

Is DoorLoop better than RentRedi?

For managing other owners' properties or needing full owner accounting, yes. For an owner-landlord who wants the lowest price, RentRedi at about $144 a year on the annual plan is hard to beat.

Is RentRedi cheaper than DoorLoop?

Almost always. RentRedi is a flat $12 a month on the annual plan with unlimited units. DoorLoop One starts at $2.50 per unit per month plus a minimum spend DoorLoop does not publish.

Can RentRedi manage properties for other owners?

It is aimed at owner-landlords and is not designed for trust accounting or outside owners. For that, look at DoorLoop or Rentec Direct.

Managing for other owners?

DoorLoop includes owner accounting and the easiest onboarding we have seen.
