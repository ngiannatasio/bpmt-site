AppFolio Alternatives (2026): Options Below the 50-Unit Minimum

Research-based guide by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

AppFolio is built for property management companies, and it prices like it: quotes only, a minimum monthly spend, and a 50-unit minimum on its Core plan. If you are below that size, or simply do not want enterprise pricing, these are the alternatives worth a look.

The short version

Under 50 units: DoorLoop. Small PM company that needs trust accounting cheaply: Rentec Direct. Established PM company that wants AppFolio-style depth: Buildium Growth or Premium.

Why people look elsewhere

  • Size minimum: AppFolio's Core plan carries a 50-unit minimum and a minimum spend.
  • Opaque pricing: AppFolio no longer publishes per-unit prices; you get a quote.
  • More than you need: below roughly 200 units, much of AppFolio's automation goes unused.

DoorLoop

No unit minimum, published pricing, and accounting that covers owner statements. Pro adds multi-entity accounting and QuickBooks sync. For most operators under 50 units it covers what AppFolio would, at a known price. See DoorLoop pricing.

Buildium

The closest like-for-like option for an established management company, with trust accounting, owner portals and phone support from the Growth plan. Compare it in DoorLoop vs Buildium vs AppFolio.

Rentec Direct

The PM edition includes trust accounting and an owner portal from about $50 a month, scaling with units. Less polished, much cheaper. See Rentec Direct vs Buildium.

Hemlane

A different model: software plus optional repair coordination and 24/7 emergency support, priced per unit. Useful if your bottleneck is maintenance calls rather than accounting.
