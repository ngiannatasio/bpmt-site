Innago Review: Free Landlord Software With No Paid Tiers

Research-based review by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

Innago’s pitch is simple: property management software that costs landlords nothing, with no paid tiers to upgrade into. This review is built from Innago's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, compared against the platforms we cover on this site. We have not run our own portfolio on it.

3.5 /5

The short version

Genuinely free for landlords, with a reasonable feature set for small portfolios. Tenants carry the payment fees, and some users report delays on early payouts, so plan a buffer.

What Innago does

Innago covers online rent collection, leases and e-signatures, tenant screening, maintenance requests and basic income and expense tracking. Revenue comes from tenant-side fees rather than landlord subscriptions.

Strengths and weaknesses

What works

  • Free for landlords, no tiers to compare
  • Card fee lower than several competitors
  • Covers the essentials for small portfolios
  • No contract

Where it falls short

  • Tenants pay transaction fees by default
  • Some reports of rent held during security reviews on early payments
  • Accounting is basic
  • Fewer independent reviews than bigger platforms

Innago is a solid free option. Just set up a payment buffer for your first month or two in case payouts are held for review.

Frequently asked questions

Is Innago free?

Yes, for landlords: Innago advertises no monthly fee, no setup fee and no contract. Tenants and applicants pay transaction and screening fees by default.

What does Innago charge tenants?

Independent reviews report about $2 per ACH payment and 2.99% for cards, paid by tenants by default; landlords can choose to absorb them. Screening is reported at about $30–$35 per applicant.

Innago or TurboTenant?

Both are free to start. Innago has no paid tiers at all; TurboTenant sells inexpensive upgrades with accounting on Pro. See our TurboTenant review for details.
