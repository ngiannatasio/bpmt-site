Property Management Software for Companies Managing 50+ Units

Written by an active real estate operator · Last updated Oct 2026

At this size, your software is infrastructure. It holds owner money, drives your reporting, and shapes how many units each staff member can handle. Switching later means migrating years of ledgers, so the cost of getting it wrong is measured in months, not dollars.

The short version

50 to a few hundred units, managing for outside owners: Buildium. 200+ units and growing as a true management company: AppFolio. 50 to 100 units of mostly your own properties, simple ownership: DoorLoop can still be the cheapest workable option.

What matters at this size

  1. Trust accounting and owner statements. If you hold other people's money, separate trust accounting and clean monthly owner statements are not optional.
  2. Reporting you can hand to owners and lenders. Customizable reports, rent rolls and delinquency reports without exporting to spreadsheets.
  3. Staff permissions and workflows. Multiple users with the right access, and maintenance and leasing workflows that do not depend on one person's inbox.
  4. Integrations. Banking, screening, accounting exports and maintenance vendors. Fewer manual handoffs means fewer errors.
  5. Migration support. Ask exactly what the vendor will import for you, and what you will have to rebuild.

Buildium: the safe middle choice

Buildium is built for growing portfolios and third-party management, with strong accounting depth, owner reporting and a large integration set. The trade-off is a steeper learning curve than newer tools. For most companies between roughly 20 and 500 units, it is the default to beat.

AppFolio: for real management companies

AppFolio is designed for management companies at scale, with automation and reporting to match, and it has unit minimums and minimum spend. Below roughly 200 units it is usually more than you need; above that, it is excellent.

DoorLoop: when simplicity still wins

If most of the units are yours and your ownership structure is simple, DoorLoop's lower price and faster setup can still beat the bigger platforms at 50 to 100 units. Watch for the ceiling: advanced reporting and complex multi-entity structures are thinner. Read the full DoorLoop review →
