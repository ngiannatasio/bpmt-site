DoorLoop vs AppFolio (2026): Which Fits Under 200 Units?

Research-based comparison by an active real estate operator · Last updated Nov 2026

AppFolio is built for property management companies; DoorLoop is built for landlords and small to mid-size managers. Both now publish per-unit starting prices and both apply a minimum spend, so the real dividing line is portfolio size. Our author has used DoorLoop in his own operation; the AppFolio side is based on AppFolio's published pricing and features plus independent reviews, not hands-on use.

The short version

Under 50 units: DoorLoop, since AppFolio's Core plan has a 50-unit minimum. 50 to about 200 units: get quotes from both; DoorLoop is easier to run. 200+ units as a management company: AppFolio is built for you.

Where AppFolio wins

Scale. AppFolio's automation and reporting are designed for companies running hundreds or thousands of units, and at that size its per-unit starting price on Core is lower than DoorLoop One's. If you run a true management company with staff, AppFolio is built for how you work. See AppFolio alternatives.

Where DoorLoop wins

Everyone below that. There is no 50-unit floor like AppFolio Core's, prices are on the website, and onboarding is the easiest we have seen. DoorLoop One includes the accounting depth, API and unlimited users that used to sit behind its top plan. Read the DoorLoop review →

The cost math

At 50 units, AppFolio Core starts at about $75 a month and DoorLoop One at about $125, both before their minimum spends. At 300 units, Core starts around $447 a month and DoorLoop One around $550 if its $1.50 rate applies above 100 units. Below 50 units AppFolio Core is not an option at all. DoorLoop now prices per unit, and every plan carries a minimum spend that DoorLoop does not publish, so get a quote for your unit count.

The verdict

If you are under 50 units, the choice is made for you: DoorLoop. Between 50 and roughly 200, DoorLoop is usually the simpler, faster system to run, so get both quotes. Above 200 units with a staffed management company, AppFolio earns its place.

Frequently asked questions

Does AppFolio have a unit minimum?

Yes. AppFolio's Core plan has a 50-unit minimum and a minimum spend; Plus and Max also list minimums. DoorLoop applies a minimum spend too but does not publish a 50-unit floor.

Is AppFolio cheaper than DoorLoop?

Per unit at scale, AppFolio Core's starting price ($1.49) is lower than DoorLoop One's ($2.50). Both add minimum spends, and AppFolio is not available below 50 units on Core, so compare quotes for your size.

Is DoorLoop a good AppFolio alternative?

For landlords and managers under about 200 units, yes: published pricing, full accounting and much easier onboarding.

Under AppFolio's minimum?

DoorLoop gives you full accounting and owner statements, with prices from $2.50 a unit listed on its own site.
